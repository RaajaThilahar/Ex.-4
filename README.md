**AI-Powered Industrial Automation Monitoring System**

This system automates manufacturing processes using IoT devices, machine learning, and embedded controllers. It performs equipment monitoring, predictive maintenance, alert generation, and efficiency tracking.

# **Procedure**

---

## **1. Define the Scenario and Use Case**

### **Scenario:**

The manufacturing industry aims to reduce manual supervision and increase automation efficiency. An AI-powered monitoring system uses IoT sensors to track equipment health, predict failures, and optimize energy usage.

### **Target Audience:**

Automotive industries, electronics assembly units, large-scale food processing plants, and factories using heavy machinery.

### **Main Objectives:**

• Increase production efficiency by **30%**
• Reduce machinery downtime through **predictive maintenance**
• Enable **real-time monitoring** of equipment
• Reduce **energy consumption** through optimized planning

---

## **2. Identify Prompt Patterns for Each Design Aspect**

---

### **Comparative Analysis Prompt**

**Definition:** Asking for a comparison between two or more concepts to highlight similarities and differences.

**Prompt:**
“Compare predictive maintenance and scheduled maintenance in industrial automation.”

---

### **Experiential Perspective Prompt**

**Definition:** Asking for an answer from a specific person’s experience or role.

**Prompt:**
“As a factory technician, describe how AI-based monitoring helps you detect machine problems earlier.”

---

### **Everyday Functioning Prompts**

**Definition:** Prompts that explain how systems work in daily life.

**Prompt:**
“Explain how industrial robots assist in everyday manufacturing tasks on an assembly line.”

---

### **Universal Prompt Structures**

**Definition:** Standard prompt templates used for any subject.

**Prompt:**
“Explain the process of predictive maintenance step-by-step.”

---

### **Prompt Refinements**

**Definition:** Improving prompt clarity and specificity.

**Poor Prompt:**
“Explain industrial automation.”

**Refined Prompt:**
“Explain industrial automation by describing sensors, controllers, communication systems, and an example production workflow.”

---

### **Prompt Size Limitations**

**Definition:** Managing long responses by breaking them into smaller sections.

**Large Prompt:**
“Describe all types of industrial IoT sensors in detail.”

**Refined Prompt:**
“List 5 common industrial IoT sensors and explain each in 3–4 lines.”

---

# **3. AI SYSTEM OUTPUTS (ChatGPT vs Gemini)**

---

## **A. Comparative Analysis Prompt**

### **Prompt:**

“Compare predictive maintenance and scheduled maintenance in industrial automation.”

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

Predictive maintenance uses sensor data, vibration analysis, and AI models to detect early signs of failure. Scheduled maintenance follows fixed time intervals regardless of machine condition. Predictive maintenance reduces downtime and cost, while scheduled maintenance is simpler but less efficient.

#### **Gemini Output:**

Predictive maintenance monitors live machine conditions using IoT and flags issues before breakdown. Scheduled maintenance is time-based and doesn’t depend on equipment health. Predictive maintenance saves more resources but requires advanced technology.

---

## **B. Experiential Perspective Prompt**

### **Prompt:**

“As a factory technician, describe how AI-based monitoring helps you detect machine problems earlier.”

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

As a technician, AI dashboards show temperature, vibration, and load changes instantly. Alerts notify me when a machine behaves abnormally, allowing me to fix issues before breakdown.

#### **Gemini Output:**

AI tools give me early warnings by displaying unusual sensor readings. I can track equipment in real time and prevent failures before they affect production.

---

## **C. Everyday Functioning Prompt**

### **Prompt:**

“Explain how industrial robots assist in everyday manufacturing tasks.”

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

Industrial robots handle repetitive tasks like welding, picking, packing, and assembly. They improve accuracy, reduce fatigue, and speed up production.

#### **Gemini Output:**

Robots perform daily activities such as material movement, product assembly, and quality checks. They help reduce manual errors and boost productivity.

---

## **D. Universal Prompt Structure**

### **Prompt:**

“Explain the process of predictive maintenance step-by-step.”

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

1. Sensors collect machine data
2. Data is analyzed by AI
3. System detects anomalies
4. Alerts are sent to technicians
5. Maintenance is scheduled before breakdown

#### **Gemini Output:**

Step 1: Monitor equipment using sensors
Step 2: Compare readings with normal values
Step 3: AI predicts risk of failure
Step 4: Maintenance teams receive notifications
Step 5: Repairs are done proactively

---

## **E. Prompt Refinement**

### **Prompt:**

“Explain industrial automation by describing sensors, controllers, communication, and workflow.”

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

Industrial automation uses sensors for data collection, controllers like PLCs for decision-making, and communication protocols for device interaction. The workflow starts with sensing, processing, action, and feedback.

#### **Gemini Output:**

Automation systems rely on sensors, PLCs, and communication modules. They interact in a cycle: sensing → processing → controlling → monitoring.

---

#### **ChatGPT Output:**

Provides 5 sensors with short descriptions.

#### **Gemini Output:**

Also provides 5 sensors with concise explanations.
